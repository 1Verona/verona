It all started…

Video Editing

MY JOURNEY / 2018 → NOW

It started with editing for fun.

  1. 2018 / THE START

    Highlights, montages & curiosity.

    I started editing for fun in 2018, learning through editing and VFX content and putting those ideas into gaming highlights, montages and short films for school. Experimenting with different formats helped me develop my eye for pacing, storytelling and visual detail.

    Tools used
  2. 2024—2025

    Prota Games

    At Prota Games, I edited gaming content across multiple channels, with a focus on Bulky Bacon. Beyond the edits themselves, I helped build the visual language and production workflow, mentored another editor and integrated AI voice production. The channel reached 200K subscribers and nearly 80M organic views in around two months through a collaborative production effort. Making the process more organized and efficient was part of the work from the start.

    Tools used
  3. 2026 / FALCON MEDIA

    Vlogs, sports, news & motion graphics.

    At Falcon Media, I worked with vlogs, sports, news content and motion graphics. Moving between these formats meant adapting the pacing and visual approach to each story, while keeping process optimization in focus: finding better ways to organize the work and make production more efficient.

    Tools used
  4. 2026 / DTC & UGC

    Arbitrage Agency & Kinectic Apps

    At Arbitrage Agency and Kinectic Apps, I worked on direct-to-consumer (DTC) ads and UGC-style creatives. This brought a different focus to the edit: communicating the product clearly and shaping the material into an engaging ad. Alongside the creative work, I kept looking for ways to streamline production and improve the workflow.

    Tools used

THE COMMON THREAD

Better processes. More room to create.

Across all of these experiences, optimizing processes has been a constant priority. I look at both the final video and the way it gets made: how the work is organized, where time is lost and how tools and AI can support production. For me, a better workflow creates more room for creative decisions and attention to detail.

MULTIPLE BRANDS

Motion graphics agent

I created and trained a motion graphics agent for multiple brands. It builds editable animations with code, adapting typography, pacing and visual style to each brand’s identity.

END-TO-END AUTOMATION

Autonomous video editing

I built an agent-orchestrated production pipeline for UGC-style and direct-response ads. It connects briefing, footage selection, editing, motion, sound and captions through to delivery. I direct the creative decisions and approve the final result.

CUSTOM TOOLS

A connected creative operation

I develop tools for captions, quality checks and delivery, supported by structured brand knowledge. A custom dashboard brings agent activity and production progress together so I can oversee the workflow.

I’m developing a system to automatically generate ad creatives for digital marketing, with a focus on UGC-style ads and direct-to-consumer (DTC) campaigns.
